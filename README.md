## 🌐 How to Deploy to Vercel for Free (2 Minutes)

1. Open [vercel.com](https://vercel.com) and log in with your GitHub or Google account.
2. Click **"Add New..."** -> **"Project"**.
3. Drag and drop the `client` folder directly into Vercel, OR push it to a private GitHub repository and import it.
4. Click **Deploy**.
5. Within 20 seconds, Vercel gives you your live link (e.g. `https://forever-leena.vercel.app`)!
6. Open `heart-qr.html`, paste your new Vercel link, and download/print your Heart QR Code!
